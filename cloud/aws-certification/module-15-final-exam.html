Sixteen Modules. One Shield, Actually Built.

Six domains, six threads, one coherent way of thinking about AWS security.

The six threads

Least Privilege

Every identity does exactly what it needs and nothing more. The most common door an attacker walks through is over-permission.

Defense-in-Depth

No single control is the only thing between your data and a breach. Stack controls at network, compute, and application layers.

Encryption Everywhere

Data at rest, in transit, in use. KMS, ACM, CloudHSM, the AWS Encryption SDK — each with its own responsibility model.

Shared Responsibility

Every service sits somewhere on the IaaS-to-managed spectrum. Knowing where changes everything about who owns the risk.

Multi-Account Architecture

Organizations, SCPs, RCPs, Control Tower — the governance layer that makes scale safe.

Blast Radius Minimization

Assume breach. Limit the damage. Separate accounts, scoped roles, short-lived credentials — limit how far failure can spread.

The real test isn't the exam score. It's whether, when you sit in the exam room, you find yourself reasoning through a scenario the way an AWS security architect does — not pattern-matching memorized answers. That's what sixteen modules were built to produce.

Exam-day advice

🛑

Don't cram in the final 48 hours

New material competes with what you already know and increases anxiety without improving retention. The preparation is done.

😴

Sleep the night before

Even mild sleep deprivation measurably degrades recall and reasoning — exactly the two things this exam tests. This is not optional.

⏰

Arrive or log in early

In-person: 30 minutes. Online proctoring: 15 minutes for room scan and system check. Buffer time removes stress.

📖

Read every question to the end

The last sentence often changes the constraint — "with least operational overhead", "without modifying existing IAM policies". Miss it and you miss the question.

🏁

Flag and return

170 minutes for 65 questions = ~2.5 min each. If a question takes more than 3 minutes, make your best guess, flag it, and move on.

Schedule the exam. If you haven't booked your slot yet, do it now. A booking date turns preparation into a deadline — and deadlines are the only thing that make preparation real.
