Mentor-Recommended Career Paths

Strategic, not random

With IT experience, skip the Foundational tier and target the Associate level directly. The most powerful career move is a strong first associate cert + one targeted specialisation backed by real hands-on projects. Two strategic certs beat collecting six at random every time.

01
☁️ Cloud Architect Track
Most universally demanded path. Highest long-term ROI. Opens Senior Architect, Principal Engineer, and Enterprise Cloud Lead roles globally.
SAA-C03→ SAP-C02→ SCS-C03
02
🤖 AI + Cloud Track
Most future-proof path for 2026+. Positions you at the intersection of cloud and generative AI — where salaries and hiring are surging fastest.
AIF-C01→ SAA-C03→ MLA-C01→ AIP-C01
03
⚙️ DevOps / Platform Track
For engineers in CI/CD, infrastructure-as-code, and automation. High demand at tech companies, scale-ups, and SRE-heavy organisations.
SAA-C03→ DVA-C02→ DOP-C02
04
🔒 Security Specialist Track
Recession-proof path. Cloud security is the most durable and highest-compensated specialisation in enterprise IT. Note: current exam is SCS-C03.
SAA-C03→ SCS-C03→ ANS-C01

🎓 Top Mentor Recommendation — July 2026

Start with SAA-C03 regardless of your target specialisation. It is the most universally recognised AWS credential and the gateway to every advanced path. Pair study with at least one real AWS project — a live deployment, a free-tier build, or a Skill Builder Jam. Real projects + strategic certs + documented outcomes will always outperform six untested credentials on a résumé.
