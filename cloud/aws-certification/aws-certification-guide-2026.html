Mentor-Recommended Career Paths

Strategic, not random

As a professional with IT experience, you can skip the Foundational tier and target the Associate level directly. The most powerful career move is a strong first associate cert + one targeted specialisation backed by real hands-on projects. Two strategic certs beat collecting six random ones every time.

🎓 Top Mentor Recommendation — July 2026

Start with SAA-C03 regardless of your target specialisation. It is the most universally recognised AWS credential and the gateway to every advanced path. Pair your study with at least one real AWS project — a live deployment, a personal portfolio on AWS, or a hands-on lab via AWS Skill Builder. Employers increasingly look for evidence alongside certifications. Real projects + strategic certs + documented outcomes will always outperform six untested credentials on a résumé.
