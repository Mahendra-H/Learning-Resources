Free Learning Resources

Every resource below is freely accessible. No paywall. No signup required for most. Handpicked by senior practitioners — these are the references bookmarked on every security professional's browser.

Threat Intelligence & News
🛡️ Government ✓ Free
CISA Cybersecurity Advisories
Real-time alerts on actively exploited vulnerabilities, nation-state threats, and critical infrastructure attacks. The primary US government feed trusted by every enterprise SOC.
cisa.gov ↗
⚔️ Framework ✓ Free
MITRE ATT&CK
The definitive knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques. Every SOC analyst, threat hunter, and red teamer uses this daily. Learn how real attackers operate — technique by technique.
attack.mitre.org ↗
📰 News ✓ Free
Krebs on Security
Brian Krebs — former Washington Post security reporter — delivers the most cited independent cybersecurity journalism. Deep investigative pieces on breaches, cybercrime, and identity theft ecosystems.
krebsonsecurity.com ↗
Standards & Frameworks
📋 Standard ✓ Free
NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0
The gold standard framework adopted by thousands of organisations globally. Organises security into six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. Identity runs through all six.
nist.gov ↗
🔑 IAM ✓ Free
NIST SP 800-63 — Digital Identity Guidelines
The definitive US government standard for identity proofing, authentication, and federation. If you work in IAM, this is your bible. Covers MFA levels, credential strength, and assurance levels.
csrc.nist.gov ↗
🌐 AppSec ✓ Free
OWASP Top 10
The most referenced application security standard in the world. Broken Access Control is #1 — an identity and authorisation problem. Essential reading for every engineer building software.
owasp.org ↗
Learning & Practice
🎮 Hands-On ✓ Free tier
TryHackMe
Browser-based cybersecurity labs with guided learning paths. No setup required. Covers SOC analysis, penetration testing, IAM attacks, and defensive techniques. Perfect for building practical skills.
tryhackme.com ↗
🎓 Research ✓ Free
SANS Reading Room
Over 2,000 peer-reviewed security research papers, whitepapers, and practitioner guides. Covering every topic in this course — SOC operations, IAM, threat hunting, GRC, cloud security — in depth.
sans.org ↗
🗺️ Reference ✓ Free
Paul Jerimy — Security Certification Roadmap
The most-shared visual map of every cybersecurity certification, organised by domain and seniority. Plan your entire career path from entry-level to CISO in one diagram.
pauljerimy.com/security-certification-roadmap ↗
IAM · Identity · Zero Trust
🔐 IAM ✓ Free
IDPro Body of Knowledge
The professional association for identity practitioners. Free peer-reviewed articles covering IAM architecture, IGA, PAM, federation, and workforce identity — written by practitioners for practitioners.
idpro.org ↗
🛡️ Zero Trust ✓ Free
NIST Zero Trust Architecture (SP 800-207)
The foundational US federal zero trust standard. Identity is the primary control plane in zero trust — this document explains why, and what mature zero trust identity architecture looks like.
csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-207 ↗
☁️ Cloud IAM ✓ Free
Microsoft Identity Platform Docs
Microsoft's comprehensive free documentation covering Entra ID, OAuth 2.0, OIDC, SAML federation, Conditional Access, and SCIM provisioning. The most detailed cloud identity reference available.
learn.microsoft.com/identity ↗

All links open in a new tab. Resources verified as of 2024–2025. The cybersecurity landscape changes rapidly — always cross-reference with primary sources and check for updated versions.
