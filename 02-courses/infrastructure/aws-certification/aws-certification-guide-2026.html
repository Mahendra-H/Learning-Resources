Mentor-Recommended Career Paths

Strategic, not random

As a professional with IT experience, you're positioned to skip the Foundational tier and go straight to the Associate level. The most powerful career move is a strong first associate cert + one targeted specialization. Two strategic certs with real hands-on projects will outperform six random certs every time. Below are the four most viable career tracks in 2026.

01
Cloud Architect Track
Most universally demanded path. Opens Senior Architect, Principal Engineer, and Enterprise Cloud Lead roles globally.
SAA-C03→ SAP-C02→ SCS-C02
02
AI + Cloud Track
Most future-proof path for 2026+. Positions you at the intersection of cloud and generative AI — where industry investment is surging.
AIF-C01→ SAA-C03→ MLA-C01→ GAD-C01
03
DevOps / Platform Track
For engineers in CI/CD, infrastructure-as-code, and automation. High demand at tech companies and scale-ups.
SAA-C03→ DVA-C02→ DOP-C02
04
Security Specialist Track
Recession-proof path. Cloud security is the most durable and highest-compensated cloud specialization in enterprise IT.
SAA-C03→ SCS-C02→ ANS-C01
🎓 Top Mentor Recommendation for July 2026

Start with SAA-C03 (Solutions Architect Associate) — it is the single most recognized and ROI-positive AWS certification regardless of your target role. It unlocks every advanced track, is required by most enterprise job postings, and delivers a $12k+ average salary boost. Once you hold it, choose your next cert based on the specific role you want — not what sounds impressive in isolation. Real AWS projects + strategic credentials + documented outcomes beats a shelf of untested certifications every time.
