🧱 The One Diagram That Ties Everything Together

Every playbook in this series keeps coming back to the same eight-layer map — we call it the AI Stack. Think of it like the OSI model, but for AI systems: each layer depends on the one below it, each has its own failure modes, its own cost profile, and its own vendors. When a topic feels confusing, the fastest way to re-orient is to ask: which layer of the stack am I actually talking about right now?

📱 Applications → 🤖 Agents → 🔧 Tools / MCP → 🧠 LLMs → 🔢 Embeddings → 🗄️ Vector DB → 📊 Data → ⚡ Infrastructure
🔵 Analogy — It's Your Enterprise Identity Stack, Mirrored
If you've ever drawn the "stack" for an enterprise IAM platform — user-facing portals on top, down through SSO/federation, down through directory services, down through the physical identity stores and the infrastructure they run on — you've already drawn a diagram with exactly this shape. A problem at the "Applications" layer (a confusing login screen) often has its real root cause three layers down (a misconfigured directory sync). AI systems behave the same way: a chatbot that "gives wrong answers" (Applications) might actually have a problem in its retrieval layer (Vector DB) or its underlying data (Data). This stack diagram will re-appear, lit up layer-by-layer, throughout every playbook — it's your "you are here" marker. 📍

🧭 How To Use This (Pick Your Path)

This is built to be read like a magazine, not a textbook — skim, jump around, come back later. But if you want a plan, here are two honest paths:

🐇 The Fast Path
~10-11 hours total

Read every module's "What is it / Why / When / How / Architect's Take" sections and all 🟢🟠🔵🟣 callouts. Skip the "🟣 Deep Dive" collapsible sections and skim the labs. You'll come out conversational on every topic in the success criteria — just with fewer war stories.

🐢 The Full Tour
~17-19 hours total

Everything — deep dives, every analogy, every lab, the full Career Paths appendix. Best if you're prepping to actually lead an AI initiative (not just sit in on the discussion) or you just enjoy this stuff. Spread over 2-3 weekends, it doesn't feel like homework. ☕

💡 Tip: the dark/light toggle (top-right of every page) is per-page — pick whichever is easier on your eyes for long reading sessions; both are fully designed, not an afterthought.
