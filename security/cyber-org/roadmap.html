The Framework That Changes Everything

Three words. Every team maps to one of them.

Before we go any further, I need to give you the mental model that will make everything else in this course click instantly.

01

Protect

Stop attacks before they happen.

These teams build the walls. Design the rules. Harden the systems. When a Protect team does its job perfectly, attacks fail before they begin.

Security Architecture IAM / IGA / PAM Cloud Security AppSec Vulnerability Management GRC & Compliance
02
02

Detect

Find attacks already in progress.

These teams watch everything. Every login. Every file touch. Every network packet. They look for the signal inside the noise.

SOC Threat Intelligence Threat Hunting
03
03

Respond

Contain the damage when attacks land.

When detection triggers, these teams activate. Their job: stop the bleeding, preserve evidence, restore operations. Every second costs money.

Incident Response Digital Forensics Crisis Management

Learner

Wait — so IAM appears under Protect. But in a breach, doesn't IAM also respond? Like — revoking credentials, cutting off access?

Mentor

You just asked the question that separates engineers from security architects.

Yes. IAM touches all three pillars.

It protects — by defining who can access what. It detects — by logging every access event. It responds — by revoking access in minutes.

This is why we have an entire dedicated module on IAM, IGA, and PAM.

Identity is not a pillar. Identity is the foundation all three pillars are built on.

"Identity is not one piece of the puzzle.
Identity is what the puzzle is made of."

Every Protect decision involves access rights. Every Detect signal involves user behavior. Every Respond action starts with revoking a credential.

This is why IAM Architects are among the most sought-after professionals in enterprise security.
