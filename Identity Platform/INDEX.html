Phase overview

4 phases, one continuous journey

Each phase builds directly on the last. You cannot architect what you haven't understood at depth. The sequence is deliberate.

Phase 0 · 2 modules
Foundations
Reframe product experience into ecosystem thinking. Build the vocabulary and mental model that every subsequent module depends on.
M01Enterprise IAM ecosystem
M02AuthN, AuthZ & lifecycle fundamentals
Effort: 8–14 hrs · Outcome: Ecosystem thinking
Phase 1 · 6 modules
Core protocols
Master the protocols, credentials, directories, and infrastructure authentication that the enterprise identity stack is built on.
M03aOAuth 2.0 & OIDC (architect depth)
M03bSAML 2.0 & federation patterns
M04aMFA, passwordless & adaptive auth
M04bDirectory services & identity stores
M05Infrastructure identity (network & Linux)
Effort: 30–46 hrs · Outcome: Protocol mastery
Phase 2 · 6 modules
Governance & PAM
Authorization architecture, enterprise IGA, privileged access, machine identity, and secrets management — the governance layer of enterprise IAM.
M06Authorization architecture
M07IGA — Saviynt & SailPoint
M08PAM — CyberArk architecture
M09aMachine identity & workload identity
M09bSecrets management, PKI & certs
Effort: 34–51 hrs · Outcome: Governance mastery
Phase 3 · 7 modules
Modern frontier
Cloud identity, identity security, platform engineering, integration patterns, reference architectures, leadership communication, and the adversarial capstone.
M10Cloud identity (AWS, Entra, GCP)
M11Identity security & zero trust
M12IAM platform engineering
M13–M16Integration → Reference Arch → Leadership → Capstone
Effort: 35–52 hrs · Outcome: Architect-level synthesis
